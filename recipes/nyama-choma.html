Nyama Choma

Description

Nyama Choma, which literally means "burnt meat" in Swahili, is Kenya's most iconic dish and a national institution. It is slow-grilled goat or beef over open charcoal, seasoned simply with salt, and eaten with your hands alongside ugali and kachumbari. It transcends tribe, class, and region — from Nairobi's Carnivore restaurant to a roadside jua kali grill in Kisumu or Mombasa, nyama choma is the shared language of Kenyan celebration.

The beauty of nyama choma is its honesty. The meat does all the talking. Good charcoal, patience, and quality goat meat are the only true requirements. Kachumbari — a bright, fresh salad of tomatoes, onions, and coriander — is the essential accompaniment that cuts through the richness of the meat.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg goat meat or beef ribs, bone-in and cut into large pieces
  • 2 tsp coarse salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Charcoal for grilling
  • For Kachumbari:
  • 3 medium ripe tomatoes, finely diced
  • 1 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 green chilli, finely sliced (optional)
  • Handful of fresh coriander, chopped
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Remove the meat from the fridge at least 30 minutes before cooking to bring it to room temperature.
  2. Rub the meat generously with coarse salt, black pepper, and lemon juice. Keep it simple — this is traditional.
  3. Light your charcoal and allow it to reach a steady, glowing heat with white ash on the coals. Avoid flames directly touching the meat.
  4. Place the meat on the grill over medium-high heat. Do not rush this process.
  5. Grill for 15 to 20 minutes per side, turning only once or twice. The goal is a deep, slightly charred crust with juicy interior.
  6. While the meat grills, prepare the kachumbari: combine diced tomatoes, red onion, chilli, and coriander in a bowl. Add lemon juice and salt. Toss and set aside.
  7. Test the meat by cutting into the thickest piece — it should be cooked through with no pink near the bone.
  8. Rest the meat for 5 minutes off the heat before serving.
  9. Serve on a wooden board with kachumbari on the side and ugali or fries.
